# Scope and limits

The primary planner supports one to thirty days and up to seven city stays. The globe, day islands, list, calendar and right-side day schedule read one canonical trip. Reviewed Qloo creation, explicit day suggestions, Explore, Saved collections, private notes and traveler-added places connect to that state while preserving destination and selection evidence.

Confirmed cultural seeds are required for live taste recommendations. Browsing discovery candidates does not accept a visit; adding asks for a day/time/duration and validates city, overlap, entered travel constraints and protected visits. Known listed hours improve automatic drafts; unknown hours remain unknown, and a warning is not proof of holiday availability.

Conversation can stay beside the planner or open its full workspace. Text replies support Stop/Retry, preserve separate composer drafts and reject late output after context/account changes. Voice requires supported browser microphone/WebRTC capabilities and actual provider access. Proposals require explicit review before canonical changes; transcripts are not automatically saved as trip memory.

Accounts provide single-server private persistence, revision conflicts and owner-isolated Saved collections/notes. Guest drafts and local conversations are device scoped. Manual backups include validated metadata; device-local media bytes are not server-synchronized. Public itinerary snapshots exclude private media and receipts. Account recovery and live collaborative editing are not provided.

No room inventory, hotel quote, booking, payment, reservation servicing, verified walking/transit schedule, complete trip cost, receipt extraction or background autonomous planning job is claimed. Sample venues are fictional. Real Qloo coverage and provider availability vary. Google imagery requires separate setup and retains a rendering fallback.

See [deployment](deployment.md) for persistence/proxy requirements and [verification](verification.md) for test boundaries.
